Mexican Hotdogs

These famous Mexican hotdogs are sauteed with onions, tomatoes, and peppers, and tucked into buttery toasted buns with melted Mexican blend cheese.

Ingredients

  • 8 Beef hotdogs
  • 3 Large jalapenos, halved
  • 3 cloves of garlic, smashed
  • 8 hotdog buns
  • 1 cup Mexican shredded cheese

Steps

  1. Make 1/8 inch slice down each hotdog
  2. cook each hotdog bun, turning occasionally
  3. Divide hotdog and vegetables evenly among toasted buns
  4. Allow cheese to melt and serve immediately
