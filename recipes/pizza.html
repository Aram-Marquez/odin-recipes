No-Yeast Pizza Crust

This no-yeast pizza dough is quick to make and bakes up into a tender and tasty crust for your favorite pizza toppings.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Baking powder
  • Salt
  • Milk
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees.
  2. Bake the crust in the preheated oven for 8 minutes.
  3. Top with your favorite toppins and bake until the crust is golden brown, about 10 to 15 minutes more.
