Cucumber and Avocato Sushi

Make avocado rolls at home with this easy vegetarian sushi recipe. Serve with wasabi, pickled ginger, and soy sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups of water
  • 1 cup white rice
  • 3 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 4 sheets nori
  • 1 medium avocado

Steps

  1. Combine water and rice and bring to a boil. reduce heat and wait 20 minutes. Set aside
  2. Cover a bamboo sushi mat with plastic grap. Spread rice evenly onto nori sheet.
  3. roll over cucumber and avocado.
  4. cut with a sharp knife until you have 6 pieces.
